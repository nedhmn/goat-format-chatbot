# Rulebook sources

Rules sources for the rulings agent, for the Goat Format era (April 2005). Each source is copied verbatim into Markdown for review before ingest. Nothing in the app reads these files yet.

`raw/` holds the plain text as extracted. The `.md` files are the reviewed copies: the source's own words, with only OCR or extraction errors, hyphenation, line breaks, page headers, footers, page numbers, and markup debris fixed.

## How each file was made

Downloads went to a scratch directory outside the repo and were only read, never run.

- `raw/rulebook-v5.txt`: `curl -L https://archive.org/download/rulebook_v05/rulebook_v05.pdf`, then `pdftotext -layout rulebook_v05.pdf`. The PDF is the original InDesign export with a real text layer, so its text is exact and needs no OCR. The archive.org OCR text (`rulebook_v05_djvu.txt`) was fetched too and used only to confirm that the source's own typos are in the printed page.
- `rulebook-v5.md`: written by hand from the PDF text (`pdftotext` in both layout and reading-order modes), then checked word for word against the PDF text with a `difflib` script. The only differences left are dropped page furniture, re-joined hyphenations (`QuickPlay` to `Quick-Play`, `faceup` to `face-up`, `and/ or` to `and/or`), heading case taken from the table of contents (`MONSTER CARDS` to `Monster Cards`), and diagram text moved into `[Diagram: ...]` lines.
- F.A.Q. snapshots: listed with the Wayback CDX API (`https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=upperdeckentertainment.com/yugioh/en/faq_advanced.aspx&output=json`), fetched with the `id_` suffix (`https://web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>id_/<url>`) to skip the Wayback toolbar.
- `raw/ude-faq-*.txt`: HTML tags stripped with Python's `html.parser`, entities decoded, whitespace collapsed. Navigation and footer text are left in.
- `ude-faq-*.md`: generated by reading each ASP.NET repeater item on the page (`lblTopic`, `lblDate`, `lblRuling` on the Advanced page; `lblQuestion`, `lblDate`, `lblAnswer` on the Basic page). Each topic or question is a `##` heading with its date line and every source line as its own paragraph. The three bold subheadings inside "THE BATTLE PHASE" on the Basic page are `###`. Checked word for word against the raw text; the only differences are the title, the source line, and the dropped navigation and "Top of page" links.

## Gaps and doubts

### Dates

- The earliest Wayback snapshot of both F.A.Q. pages is 2005-10-26, six months after April 2005. Nothing earlier exists on `upperdeckentertainment.com` or `entertainment.upperdeck.com`. The snapshot is the page as of October 2005, not April.
- Entries marked "Added June 2005" or "Revised June 2005" postdate April 2005: on the Advanced page, Battle Position Changes, Missing the Timing - Optional Trigger Effects, Unaffected by Spell Cards, and Returning Activated Cards; on the Basic page, WHAT IS A MONSTER TOKEN? (added) and WHAT IS A REPLAY? (revised). The Basic entry CAN I USE "MONSTER REBORN" ON MY "ARMED DRAGON LV7"? is marked "Added January 2005". Entries with no date may have been added or edited at any time before October 2005, so they could reflect rulings newer than April 2005.

### Garbled or uncertain text

- None unrecovered. The rulebook PDF has a real text layer, so there is no OCR damage.
- Source typos kept as printed (confirmed in the archive.org OCR): "Tribure" (Tribute Summon), "Continious Spell" (Spell Speed 1), "II. Spell Speed 3" (should read III), "These card are needed" (Ritual Spell Cards), "a series Spell or Trap Cards" (What is a Chain?).

### Omitted or reduced

- Rulebook cover, table of contents, the blank Note pages, and the back cover (contact numbers and legal notice) are omitted.
- The rulebook's chapter divider headings (1 Preparation for the Duel to 6 More Information) are not kept as headings. Each `##` is a section from the table of contents.
- Card example images are reduced to `[Diagram: ...]` lines naming the card and its callout labels. The example cards' own printed text (for example the effect text of "Relinquished" and of the DB1-EN067 Effect Monster, whose name the PDF text does not include) is omitted.
- The game mat diagram, card icons, turn flowchart, Battle Phase quick reference chart, Flow of the Battle & Damage Steps chart, and Chain example are rendered from their own text labels. The six card icon images themselves are not reproducible.
- The Fusion example ("Darkfire Dragon") has card images with no text in the PDF. Omitted.
- Inline bold and underline emphasis on the F.A.Q. pages (for example "very last thing", "not", "turn player") is dropped; the words are kept.
- The "click here" links on the F.A.Q. pages (email guidelines) are kept as plain words.

### Topics a Goat Format rules reference needs that these sources do not cover

- Priority: no source states that the turn player gets the first chance to act, or how priority passes in each phase and step.
- Damage Step timing: the rulebook and Basic F.A.Q. give the Damage Step order (flip, calculate, resolve effects, send to Graveyard) but not the later formal substeps (start of the Damage Step, before damage calculation, during damage calculation, after damage calculation, end of the Damage Step) or which effects may activate in each.
- Triggers: "Missing the Timing" covers only optional "when ... you can" Trigger Effects. Mandatory Trigger Effects, "if" versus "when" wording, and the full order for simultaneous mandatory and optional triggers (later called SEGOC) are only partly covered by "Simultaneous Effects".
- Continuous effects and ATK/DEF modifiers: no rules for applying multiple modifiers, original ATK being set by an effect, or effects that end when a card leaves the field.
- Spirit monsters (return to hand in the End Phase) have no general rule in any source.
- Chain building with conjunctions ("and", "and if you do", "then") and partial resolution when a target is gone.
- End Phase order: both sources say to discard down to 6, but neither says how that ordering interacts with End Phase effects.
- The Forbidden and Limited list in force in April 2005, which the rulebook defers to www.yugioh-card.com.
- Tournament rules: Konami Tournament Policy and the Upper Deck penalty guidelines (match length, side decking time, procedural errors) are not included.
- Duel Master's Guide (DVD): the Basic F.A.Q. names it as the second basic rules source; it is not text and is not included.
- Single-card rulings (Upper Deck "Specific Card Questions & Answers") are out of scope for this folder.
